# Example review structure

> **Template example only:** This is not a review of a real anime and does not represent Alex’s viewing history or opinions.

## Details

- **Status:** Completed
- **Episodes watched:** `[watched]/[total]`
- **Completed:** `YYYY-MM-DD`
- **Format:** Series
- **Genres:** `[genre one]`, `[genre two]`

## Spoiler-free review

Write a short overview that avoids major plot details. Explain the show’s appeal and the kind of viewer who may enjoy it.

## Highlights

- Describe a strong character, theme, production element, or story choice.
- Add another specific point in your own words.

## Drawbacks

- Describe pacing, writing, production, or personal-fit concerns.

## Final thoughts

Summarize the experience and explain the final placement.

## Rating

- **Score:** `[score]/10`
- **Tier:** `[S/A/B/C/D]`

## Optional progress notes

- `YYYY-MM-DD — Episode X:` A brief observation. Mark spoilers before including them.
